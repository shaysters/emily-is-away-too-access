A small fix for typing.

- While a reply is being typed, in a text box, or while typing your own answer, the mod's letter keys (c, t, w, l, i, r, m) and the arrow, Enter and Space keys no longer do mod things; they just type. Hold Ctrl to use them then (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+M, Ctrl+Up and so on).
- Page Up and Page Down still read back through the chat at any time, and no longer type a letter of your reply.

To update, run **EmilyIsAwayTooAccessInstaller.exe** again (or download it below), or extract **EmilyIsAwayTooAccess.zip** into the game folder.
